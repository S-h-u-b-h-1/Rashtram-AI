# Compliance Copilot V1

Compliance Copilot extends the existing problem recommender and Research Engine V3. A researcher describes one business or policy problem; the service uses catalogue recommendations, verified knowledge discovery, and bounded Retrieval V3 calls across at most five candidate documents.

The output separates potentially relevant areas, regulators, verified primary-official documents, cited obligation/permission/penalty passages, state-specific candidates, missing evidence, and questions requiring professional verification. It never converts a missing search result into a claim that a law does not apply.

Only passages that pass the existing evidence-sufficiency gate enter material findings. Results are owned by the authenticated account, deleted with that account, and labelled as research assistance rather than legal advice.
